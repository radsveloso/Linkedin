The Orphan Dashboard: The BI Problem Nobody Talks About

Your company accumulates dashboards the same way it accumulates technical debt. Quietly. Without anyone noticing. Until the day someone asks: who owns this?

Governance meeting. Someone shares their screen and pulls up a dashboard that shows up in usage reports every single week. Simple question: who owns this? Silence. Everyone looks sideways. The dashboard is two years old, generates 400 views a month, and nobody knows who built it.

This is not the exception. It is the rule in most Power BI tenants I have audited.

The solution: a 4-layer ownership architecture covering workspace governance, endorsement as a quality contract, defined Data Owner and Report Owner, and a content lifecycle with mandatory review dates.

PowerShell audit script included to inventory your entire tenant.
